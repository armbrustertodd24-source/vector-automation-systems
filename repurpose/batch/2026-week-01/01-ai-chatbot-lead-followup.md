## Script (≈40s, spoken)

- **[0:00 – Hook]** A lead fills out your form at 9:14 at night. Your competitor's system texts them back at 9:14. You text them at 9 the next morning. You already lost that case.
- **[0:06 – Problem]** Speed-to-lead is the whole game in PI. After five minutes, your odds of even reaching that person fall off a cliff — and they're filling out three other firms' forms too.
- **[0:16 – Insight]** You don't fix that by telling your intake team to "be faster." Humans sleep. You fix it by making the *first touch* instant and automatic.
- **[0:24 – How]** An AI chatbot catches the form, texts back in under a minute, asks the three qualifying questions — accident type, injury, were they at fault — and books the consult straight into the calendar. Your team wakes up to a booked case, not a cold lead.
- **[0:36 – Payoff]** Same lead spend. You just stopped lighting it on fire overnight.
