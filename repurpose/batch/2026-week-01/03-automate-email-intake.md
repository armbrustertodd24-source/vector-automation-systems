## Script (≈42s, spoken)

- **[0:00 – Hook]** Your intake coordinator spends two hours a day in the inbox sorting, copying details into the case system, and writing the same five replies. That's expensive triage work.
- **[0:10 – Problem]** And every minute an inbound case email sits unread is a minute a signed client is waiting — or wandering to another firm.
- **[0:18 – Insight]** You don't need to replace the person. You need to hand them a triage queue instead of a raw inbox.
- **[0:26 – How]** An AI agent reads each email, tags it — new case, existing client, vendor, spam — pulls the key facts into your case-management system, and drafts the reply. Your coordinator opens the inbox to drafts ready to send, not a blank page.
- **[0:37 – Payoff]** Same headcount. Two hours back. Nothing falls through the cracks.
