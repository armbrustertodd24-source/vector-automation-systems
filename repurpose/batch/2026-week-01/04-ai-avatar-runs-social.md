## Script (≈37s, spoken)

- **[0:00 – Hook]** I show up on four platforms every day and I spend about ten minutes on it — because a social manager isn't a person, it's an agent.
- **[0:09 – What it does]** Once a week it pulls trending angles in my niche, writes the posts, generates the visual, and reformats each one for the platform it's going to.
- **[0:19 – The handoff]** Then it stops. Everything lands in a review board as drafts.
- **[0:24 – My job]** I spend ten minutes editing the lines that don't sound like me and hitting approve. Approved posts schedule themselves.
- **[0:31 – Payoff]** I went from *producing* content to *editing* it. That's the whole shift — and it's the difference between burning out and being consistent.
