## Script (≈36s, spoken)

- **[0:00 – Hook]** Everyone's first automation idea is way too ambitious. You don't need an AI agent. You need to delete one annoying 20-minute task.
- **[0:08 – The shape]** Every automation is the same three parts: a trigger, an action, and a notification. Something happens, the system does the thing, it tells you it's done.
- **[0:18 – Example]** Real one: a form gets submitted — that's the trigger. The details get added to a spreadsheet and a folder gets created — that's the action. You get a message — that's the notification. Fifteen minutes of clicking, gone.
- **[0:29 – The lesson]** Ship the boring version first. You can make it smart later. Most people never ship because they're trying to build the impressive thing on day one.
