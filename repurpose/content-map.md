## How to work the backlog

1. Pick the next `▢ backlog` row, weighting toward an under-served **segment**.
2. Run `repurpose.mjs` to scaffold, then prompts #1–#4 to fill it.
3. Flip status to `◐` here and add the row to `content-calendar.md`.
4. After posting, flip to `●`.

When every row is `●`, we've genuinely repurposed her whole catalog — then loop
back for fresh videos via the `SYSTEM.md` trigger.
