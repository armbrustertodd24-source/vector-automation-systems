Everyone's first automation idea is way too ambitious. You don't need an AI agent. You need to delete one annoying 20-minute task.
Every automation is the same three parts: a trigger, an action, and a notification. Something happens, the system does the thing, it tells you it's done.
Real one: a form gets submitted — that's the trigger. The details get added to a spreadsheet and a folder gets created — that's the action. You get a message — that's the notification. Fifteen minutes of clicking, gone.
Ship the boring version first. You can make it smart later. Most people never ship because they're trying to build the impressive thing on day one.
