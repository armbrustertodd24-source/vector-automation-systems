You don't have a content problem. You have a repurposing problem. One long video is already a week of posts — you're just throwing it away.
The mistake is cutting by length — grab any 30 seconds. Instead, cut by moment: every standalone idea, hot take, or before-and-after is its own short.
One twenty-minute video usually holds five to seven real moments. Each becomes a short with its own hook. That's your week — from one recording session.
Same effort to create, five times the distribution. That gap is the entire game on short-form right now.
